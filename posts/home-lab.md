*Draft. Replace the prompts below with what actually happened. The same structure works for any project write-up.*

## What it is

One or two sentences: what runs on it and why you built it.

## How it is laid out

Describe the architecture at a high level: the machines, what each one does, how traffic flows between them. A simple diagram helps: `![network diagram](posts/img/home-lab.png)`.

## Decisions

Why this hardware, why these services, what you considered and rejected.

## What broke

The most useful section. Failed upgrades, bad configs, corrupted SD cards, DNS problems. For each one: the symptom, how you found the cause, and the fix.

## What I would change

What you would do differently on a rebuild, and what you want to add next.

> Do not publish internal IP ranges, open ports, exact versions of anything exposed to the internet, or anything else someone could use to map your network. Describe the design, not the addresses.
